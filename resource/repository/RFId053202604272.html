Electron Microscopy Public Image Archive

EMPIAR is a specialized public repository for raw, high-resolution image data. It stores the massive structural data used to generate 3D cryo-EM maps and tomograms, alongside 3D datasets produced by volume electron microscopy, as well as soft and hard X-ray tomography techniques. It serves as an essential companion archive to the Protein Data Bank (PDB) and EM Data Bank (EMDB).
